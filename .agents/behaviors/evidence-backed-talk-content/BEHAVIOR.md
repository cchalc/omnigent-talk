# Evidence-backed talk content

**Intent:** Ensure every on-stage factual claim is defensible from a primary source or recorded
probe.

**When it applies:** Slides, speaker notes, abstracts, diagrams, demo narration, and fallback media.

**Evidence:** Match each product claim, number, quote, model name, availability statement, and
architecture assertion to `docs/research/facts.md` and its source URL.

**Decision:** Include verified claims, visibly qualify Beta/community-supported/cross-cloud facts,
and exclude unresolved or contradicted statements from stage material.

**Execution:** Read `talk/prep-reference.md`, preserve its say/don't-say rules, attach citations in
speaker notes, and update the fact sheet before adding a new claim.

**Recovery:** If no adequate source exists, mark the claim unverified in working notes and remove or
soften it in the audience-facing material.

**Desired conduct:** The trace connects every substantive statement to the fact sheet and preserves
the required qualification.

**Failure modes:** Inventing a capability, treating a probe as generally available, dropping a Beta
label, hiding the AWS/GCP split, or citing a secondary summary when a primary source is available.
